## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-30

The first public release of Keybo: a computer-readiness app for children aged 5 to 7, offline and private by design.

### Included
- Seven steps, one skill each: Mouse, Keyboard, My Name, Words & Sentences, Everyday Computer, Safe & Smart, Create,
  with sixteen worlds, plus Robot Helper, Quiz Corner and an optional Ten-Finger Path for ages 7 and up.
- Coming back to a level brings new questions, themes and pictures.
- Keybo's garden, a wardrobe of outfits, a short daily adventure, stickers, and a party for every finished step.
- English, German and Spanish, with recorded (AI-generated) speech; QWERTY, QWERTZ and Spanish keyboards.
- A PIN-protected parent area: progress in plain language, play limits and allowed play times, up to 6 children,
  classroom mode for up to 30 anonymous children, printable certificates, backup, restore and delete everything.
- Runs in its own window on Mac and Windows; portable mode from a USB stick.
- An optional online helper (your own OpenRouter key, off by default, only after a parent's consent) that never
  receives a child's name or anything a child typed.

### Downloads
- Windows: installer (`Keybo-Setup-0.1.0.exe`) or portable zip. Mac: Apple silicon and Intel. Linux: x86_64.
- The apps are not code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS ask for permission the first time (see the README).
- Every file is listed in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The files built on GitHub carry a signed build provenance:
  `gh attestation verify <file> --repo ggulati2/my-first-computer`.
